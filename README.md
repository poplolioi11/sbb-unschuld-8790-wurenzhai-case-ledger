# 誤人齋醫案流水賬（柏林 Slg. Unschuld 8790） / Wu ren zhai yi an liu shui zhang [A day-to-day ledger of medical cases from Wuren Studio]: Transcription Dataset

《誤人齋醫案流水賬》為柏林國家圖書館 Slg. Unschuld 8790 藏晚清漢文手稿，外封紅籤署光緒二十八年（1902）立簿。日常記錄多次就診、出藥、沿用原方或改方及收付。可用來追查同一稱謂在不同日子的再診與出藥，以及醫案和日常帳務的關係。

Wu ren zhai yi an liu shui zhang, Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin Slg. Unschuld 8790, is a late Qing manuscript whose cover carries a ledger-opening date of 1902. Dated entries track individual consultations, repeated dispensing, revisions to earlier prescriptions and payments. It documents the relationship between follow-up care and day-to-day bookkeeping.

館方 199 幅均有定位。分錄案頭、原日期、出藥與藥欄、可辨收付和細註。朱墨、反透、裝訂與色卡重攝另列。快速草書帳碼及密排小字用量多數未逐字收錄，請從原圖核對。

All 199 scans are indexed. Case headings, original dates, dispensing and prescription columns, legible accounts and smaller notes are separated from marks, show-through and binding features. Most small cursive quantities and account codes are outside the systematic text coverage.

## 文字與覆核 / Text and review

AI 輔助初錄。保留原件缺損、疑讀及全冊影像定位。未做人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查或準確率量測。依原頁次及各組欄位提供 TXT、JSON 與 TEI，其中日期沿用原記法；「□」及疑讀保留，細量只收可辨處。

AI-assisted initial transcription with source references and marked uncertainties. Human full-text double proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement were not performed. TXT, JSON and TEI follow scan and column order. Dates use the manuscript’s notation. Unreadable and uncertain forms remain marked. Small quantities are only recorded where legible.

TXT 依幅、面與欄提供閱讀定位。JSON 和 TEI 採同一固定取文。「□」標難辨字，缺幅或不確定缺字處由頁面說明及疑義資料標示。完整提示、私有覆核過程與專用程式未隨附。AI 實際模型路由與用量未知。
